You know all about me\
Even the things I hide\
You nag\
Daily\
But you never judge

You fill up with joy\
quicker than I can manage\
you don't let me forget\
Anything\
Ever\
Even things I know nothing about

We play tag\
but you always win\
pretending I am popular\
The entire world is out to get me\
through you

You steal me away\
grabbing my attention\
with beeps and alarms\
everywhere\
I can't get away\
even if I try\
but I don't

I can't blame you\
I can't stay disconnected\
Even when I want to\
When I intentionally shut you up\
I regret\
I miss you\
The connection to the world\
The unread numbers\
that fake acclaim\
and hide loneliness\
And I suddenly remember\
There must be an app for that

* * *

This was a class exercise, where we were asked to read "[An Ode to my Socks](http://medicine.stonybrookmedicine.edu/surgery/blog/pablo-nerudas-ode-to-my-socks-as-rendered-by-william-carlos-williams-md)" by Pablo Neruda, and then write our own ode to an ordinary thing...
