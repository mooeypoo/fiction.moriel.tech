I am not very good at spilling my feelings\
On paper, for you\
I never really know\
If you like it\
Or just tell me you do\
Because it’s Valentines’ Day

It’s not that I don’t know what to say\
I am fairly articulate\
But in that particular day\
It sounds silly, and childish\
It comes out all wrong\
As if I have to be spilling my feelings\
On paper\
And you have to like it

So when Valentine’s Day comes\
I just prefer you\
Spill your feelings to me\
So I can tell you I like it\
A lot\
Even if I don’t
