No no no,\
Please, continue\
You’ve evolved to be so good at it\
Crushing curiosity\
In the name of devotion\
Blindly giving away your faith

No no, I get it\
You want to be loved\
Embraced and held\
You want me to feed you Life\
In tiny understandable bits\
Crushed in a spoon

No, the universe is full of it\
Disgusting wonder and abominable awe\
Just so you can turn away from it\
Misinterpret and mistrust\
Invent your own

Oh, If only you knew\
I really was playing dice\
With the universe\
And out came you

Yes, it’s about time\
I start saying no.

* * *

*Written as a response to the poem "[God Says Yes to Me](http://www.loc.gov/poetry/180/126.html)" by Kaylin Haught.*

*Found in [Poetry 180: A Turning Back to Poetry](https://www.amazon.com/dp/0812968875), edited by Billy Collins.*
